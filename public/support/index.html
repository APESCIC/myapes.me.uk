Immediate danger

If a person or animal is in immediate danger, call 999.

Social channels, Discord and feedback forms are not emergency welfare routes.

Data requests and complaints are handled through the MyAPES Account portal.

The public website keeps guidance easy to find, but private complaint details, identity checks, data-access requests and case-specific follow-up should be raised through secure MyAPES Account handling.
