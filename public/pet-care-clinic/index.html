Pet Care Clinic

Supportive pet care for every species.

APES Pet Care Clinic provides practical first aid support, welfare guidance, husbandry advice and referral triage for pets and carers. The public page explains the service clearly without exposing private clinical details.

Reassuring support

We speak plainly, stay non-judgmental and help owners feel calmer about what to do next.

Practical next steps

Each route explains suitability, warning signs and when veterinary referral is the safer choice.

Wide species care

Domestic pets, rabbits, reptiles, invertebrates, guinea pigs and ferrets all have dedicated care routes.

What the clinic can and cannot do

What we help with

First aid support, routine welfare guidance, husbandry advice and referral triage for a wide range of species.

What we do not do

We are not a veterinary surgery and do not prescribe veterinary medicines, perform surgery, carry out invasive procedures or provide sedation.

Payment guidance

Start with the Services Hub before paying. Payment does not confirm clinical suitability or replace a vet assessment.

Emergency care

If a pet needs urgent or emergency veterinary treatment, contact a registered veterinary surgeon immediately.

Need the live clinic pages?

The APES Pet Care Clinic website contains the detailed public service content, while MyAPES keeps the route and expectations clear.
