Terms of Service

Public information and support use on clear terms.

The public website is informational. If APES needs a private follow-up or registration process, the team will direct you separately.

Service boundaries

Public pages provide guidance, signposting and network links. Any private follow-up workflow is handled outside this public beta site.

Acceptable use

Users should provide accurate information and should not misuse registration, messaging or support systems.

Operational controls

APES CIC may suspend or restrict access if governance, safeguarding or operational concerns require it.
