Shelter & Rescue

Rescue, rehabilitate and rehome exotic animals.

APES Shelter & Rescue helps with surrender, rescue, adoption, sponsorship and practical welfare support. Start here to find the right route before moving into any protected or case-specific workflow.

Adoption and fostering

Find animals, understand suitability checks and move through the adoption journey with welfare-first guidance.

Surrender and rescue

Use the shelter route when you can no longer care for an animal or when a rescue case needs APES involvement.

Sponsorship and support

Sponsor a primate and help fund food, bedding, enrichment, enclosure care and specialist welfare support.

Start with the right route

Adopt an animal

Use the adoption route for rehoming, aftercare expectations and responsible placement checks.

Need to surrender?

Use the surrender route if you can no longer safely meet an animal's needs.

It is not your animal?

Use the rescue route for stray, escaped, abandoned or unwanted exotic animals.

Emergency or urgent case?

If an animal is injured, loose in public or at immediate risk, seek emergency, veterinary or local authority help first.

Need the live APES Shelter website?

The detailed service pages remain on the Shelter & Rescue website, while MyAPES keeps the public route and signposting clear.
