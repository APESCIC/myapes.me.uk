About

MyAPES helps people reach the right APES service first.

The site is designed as the public front door for APES CIC. It helps visitors find service routes, specialist support pages, news and guidance without needing to understand the internal setup behind the website.

What MyAPES does

Public guidance first

Visitors can find the right service, community link or contact route before they need to sign in or share private details.

Protected workflows second

Private follow-up stays off the public website while MyAPES focuses on clear public guidance and support routes.

Shared APES network

MyAPES connects people to APES Shelter & Rescue, APES Pet Care Clinic, APES Newsroom and other public services.

How the site helps

For service users

Find practical next steps, contact information and public guidance before using any separate private support route.

For supporters

Use MyAPES to reach donations, newsletters, APES Communities and the Change Log Hub from one place.

For APES services

Present Shelter & Rescue and Pet Care Clinic in language that matches their live public service pages.
