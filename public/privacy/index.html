Privacy Notice

Privacy is part of the design, not an afterthought.

MyAPES keeps public browsing simple while reserving more sensitive information, tickets and identity-linked workflows for protected routes.

Data minimisation

We only ask for information needed for support, registration, governance and welfare-related service delivery.

Protected records

Animal records, complaints, privacy requests and any private support activity belong behind protected systems rather than public pages.

Retention and accountability

Any protected workflow should be auditable and aligned with documented retention expectations.
