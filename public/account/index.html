Secure portal

MyAPES Account is separate from the public MyAPES website.

MyAPES Account is the secure portal for service users, animal welfare cases, pet profiles, support tickets, data protection requests and complaints. Use it when you need private account activity or secure records.

Open MyAPES Account, opens external website Open Resource Hub Get support

Privacy by design

The public MyAPES website does not collect, display or manage private service user records. Account, case, ticket, pet profile and private data protection actions are sent to the secure portal or an approved APES route.

Which route should I use?

Private account activity

Service user records, cases, tickets, pet profiles and private data protection support belong in the secure account portal.

Open MyAPES Account, opens external website

Use Contact Centre for routing

Enquiries, welfare, bookings and support routing should start with the Contact Centre when you are unsure.

Open Contact Centre, opens external website

Use public pages for general information

Resources, policies, updates, support pages and community routes remain public and do not require private account access.
